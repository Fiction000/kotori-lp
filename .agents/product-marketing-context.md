# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-01*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A free, beautiful iOS reader for classic Japanese literature from Aozora Bunko.
**What it does:** Kotori lets you browse, search, and read thousands of public-domain Japanese literary works from Aozora Bunko in a beautiful vertical text (tategaki) layout with furigana, an offline dictionary, highlights, and multiple reading themes. It's designed for people who want to experience Japanese literature the way it was meant to be read — vertically, beautifully, and without distractions.
**Product category:** Aozora Bunko reader / Japanese literature reader app (keywords: 青空文庫 アプリ, 青空文庫 リーダー)
**Product type:** Free native iOS app (no web app)
**Business model:** Completely free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. Kotori uses privacy-friendly anonymous usage analytics through TelemetryDeck, which does not store IP addresses or personally identifiable information. Passion project.

## Target Audience
**Target users:**
- Japanese literature lovers who use iOS
- Regular or occasional Aozora Bunko readers
- People dissatisfied with existing Aozora Bunko reader UIs and reading experiences
- Intermediate-to-advanced Japanese learners who want to read literary works in the original

**Primary use case:** Reading classic Japanese literature on iPhone/iPad in a comfortable, modern, distraction-free environment
**Jobs to be done:**
- "I want to read Aozora Bunko classics on my phone without a clunky, outdated interface"
- "I want to look up unfamiliar words while reading without switching apps"
- "I want to read in vertical text with furigana so I can enjoy the authentic reading experience and learn at the same time"
**Use cases:**
- Commute reading — pick up a classic on the train
- Language study — reading literature with dictionary and furigana support
- Literary discovery — browsing authors and works, finding new favorites
- Offline reading — downloaded books available anywhere without internet

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Existing Aozora Bunko reader apps have outdated UIs, weak reader features, and poor search — they make reading a chore instead of a pleasure.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- ソラリ: Outdated UI (tacky 3D bookshelf), can't change font size or settings from the reader screen, search limited to あかさたな index only, updates stopped in late 2024
- Yom!: Responsive but limited features (favorite authors only)
- Aozora Bunko website: No native app experience, no offline, no dictionary integration
- Generic ebook apps (Kindle, Apple Books): No Aozora Bunko integration, vertical text is an afterthought, no furigana
**What it costs them:** Friction kills the reading habit. People who want to read Japanese classics give up because the tools are clunky.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration that such a treasure trove of literature (Aozora Bunko) doesn't have a reader worthy of the content.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:**
- ソラリ (Sorari) — Most established Aozora reader. Falls short: outdated UI, weak reader customization, limited search, stale development (no updates since late 2024)
- Yom! — Lightweight Aozora reader. Falls short: limited features beyond favorites
**Secondary:**
- Reading Aozora Bunko on the web — Falls short: no offline, no dictionary, poor mobile experience
**Indirect:**
- Kindle / Apple Books — Falls short: no Aozora Bunko catalog, vertical text not native, no furigana, no integrated dictionary for Japanese

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Modern, beautiful UI inspired by Japanese design principles (ma/間, shodo/書道, okuzuke/奥付) — competitors look dated
- Fast search — find and start reading any work immediately (vs. browsing あいうえお index)
- Vertical text reader with deep customization (font, size, themes)
- Offline dictionary built from Wiktionary — tap any word to look up instantly
- Highlight and note-taking while reading
- 4 reading themes (light, dark, sepia, and more)
- Author profiles (Wikipedia-sourced) and AI-generated book introductions (no spoilers)
- Zero ads and no personal tracking; reading data stays on-device, while anonymous usage analytics help improve the app
**How we do it differently:** Purpose-built for Japanese literature, not a generic ebook shell. Every design decision — from typography (GenEiKoburiMin) to layout to color palette — serves the reading experience.
**Why that's better:** Reading Japanese literature should feel literary, not utilitarian. Kotori treats the content with the respect it deserves.
**Why customers choose us:** "It's the reader Aozora Bunko deserves" — beautiful, fast, respectful of the content and the reader.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already use ソラリ/Yom!" | Try Kotori's reader and search — the difference is immediately obvious. Modern UI, better customization, integrated dictionary. |
| "Why not just use Kindle/Apple Books?" | They don't have Aozora Bunko's catalog, and their Japanese reading experience (vertical text, furigana, dictionary) is an afterthought. |
| "How do you make money if it's free?" | It's a passion project. No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Privacy-friendly anonymous analytics help improve the app, and the code is open source on GitHub. |

**Anti-persona:**
- People who only read in English / don't read Japanese
- People looking for modern/contemporary Japanese books (Aozora Bunko is public domain classics)
- People who need cloud sync across devices (all data is local)

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with outdated UIs, clunky search, limited reader features in existing Aozora apps
**Pull:** Beautiful reading experience, instant dictionary lookup, fast search, modern design, completely free
**Habit:** Familiarity with current app, existing bookmarks/reading progress in another reader
**Anxiety:** "Will it have the same books?" (yes — same Aozora Bunko catalog), "Will my reading progress transfer?" (no — fresh start)
